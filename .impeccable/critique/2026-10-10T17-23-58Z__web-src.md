## Overall Impression

The thinking and the wording are strong. The weak spot is the 20 seconds that matter most: when the presenter clicks an action, the screen still says "critical" in red. The biggest opportunity is to make the saved money and the protected batch the largest thing on screen.

## What's Working

1. Two problems, two actions, shown honestly. The Decide table gives risk and runway their own columns and says plainly that more cooling does not stop a storm.
2. The Evidence grid with the alert line slider. One square per test shipment, redrawn from 77 to 266 false alarms as the line moves, with "flag everything: 387" as the baseline.
3. The copy. Question headings match the five judging pillars, and "Simulated shipments" is on every screen.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The payoff is small and the ending is red**
- What: after the direct flight, "Net saving $203,357" is 14 px text (styles.css 144), smaller than the $3.0M cargo figure. The 31% turns grey. After re-icing the big number returns to red 99%. After commit, a red toast for the next shipment (App.tsx 28-36) lands on the success toast, and the queue still shows SC-12132 as rank 01.
- Why it matters: this is the moment the judges score, and from the back of the room every step still looks critical.
- Fix: in the Rail (Guided.tsx 262-298) make net saving the largest number, in teal. Add a second status line "Batch protected: yes / no" that turns teal after re-icing. Suppress alert toasts while on the response view and just after a commit. Mark the queue row "Action logged".
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Too small and too similar for a projector**
- What: about 75% of text is 11-13 px (base 14 px at styles.css 38, eyebrows 11 px at line 55). Amber #b25e09 and red #d92d20 have nearly the same luminance (1.03 between them). Control borders #bfcfce on white are 1.6:1 (3:1 needed).
- Why it matters: PRODUCT.md requires projector readability and WCAG AA.
- Fix: base to 16 px, notes to 14 px, eyebrows and chart ticks to 13 px. Make amber clearly yellower. Put the words Critical / High in queue rows. Darken --muted to about #4f676b and --line-strong to about #8fa5a4.
- Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

**[P1] The Decide step does not fit a laptop screen**
- What: at about 1536 x 730, header, chips, SLA banner and stepper take about 345 px. Only 4 of 6 actions show; the runway chart and Commit button are below the fold.
- Why it matters: the presenter scrolls mid-pitch, or the judge never sees the chart change.
- Fix: collapse the SLA banner to a one-line chip, move chips onto the title line, make the rail sticky.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] Details that hurt credibility**
- What: 7 of the top 10 are road shipments across oceans with up to 1,407 hours to arrival. "Top 3 drivers" shows +53, +1, +0 points. Refrigerated ground transport says "no risk saving" although it adds 64.6 h of runway (Guided.tsx 241, `changed` true at sub-1% movement). Typo "a Elevated Procedure".
- Why it matters: a judge who spots one absurd row stops trusting the 85% precision.
- Fix: keep flag_land_route rows out of the demo queue (backend). Show only drivers above about 2 points. Compare `changed` on rounded percentages.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Keyboard and screen reader gaps**
- What: ten buttons all named "Open" (MapView.tsx 28). Alert line slider announces "3" not "60%" (App.tsx 66). Focus drops to body after each view change. The big risk number is NumberFlow inside aria-live with no label (Guided.tsx 265-267); not verified with a screen reader. No nav landmark or skip link.
- Why it matters: keyboard and screen reader users cannot tell rows apart or hear the result.
- Fix: aria-label with the shipment ID, aria-valuetext on the slider, focus the h1 on view change, wrap nav in <nav>.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit
